absolute zero
the air trembles
where your name should be.
i reach into the dark
and pull back nothing but coldness
from someone that isn’t there.
your shadow clings
to the walls of my sleep.
frost gathers in your shape,
and every heartbeat cracks
a little more of the dark.
beneath my skin,
the ache slows to ice.
even grief forgets to move.
i wait for the world to end,
but it already has.
my hands forget warmth,
my voice forgets sound.
i am a spectre, caught in ice,
a thought that never finished
its falling—
frozen in a moment
of being absolute zero.
