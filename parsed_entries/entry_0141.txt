Demons
I sit staring at the blackened sky
Wondering if it reflects my soul
A thousand lifetimes have come to pass
Drawn into the spite of this black hole
Demons whisper in a voice so sweet
Their sharpened tongues tell loving lies
Fastidious to be made inculpable of blame
And be held unaccountable for that which dies
Dizzying blackness, all that I sense
All hope has died, there is nothing left
Can there be anything, really
In a world that has been love-bereft
So, sleep now, and let come what dreams may
For tomorrow we are the dearly departed
And we accept this fate with ease
For the world does not love the broken-hearted
