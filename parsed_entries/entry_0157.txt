The Shape That Spoke

I spoke to hear the echo fade,
to prove the hollow still obeyed.
My name returned, but not the same:
a blurred reflection whispering flame.

The mirror learned to speak instead,
repeating things I almost said.
Each word a spark, then brief release,
unraveling what once was peace.

The air grew still, my voice grew thin,
and silence settled deeper in.
I wore it like remembered skin,
forgetting where I might have been.

The dark light hummed, a patient hymn,
its rhythm folding into dim;
the shape I was, the thought once mine,
dissolved like thought within design.

No grief remained, no need to mend,
just rhyme without a meaning’s end.
A pattern breathing once, then gone—
the song forgot, the silence drawn.
