Blood was on his face and hands, but he didn't know how it got there. Where was he anyway? Somewhere out on the street. Which one, he had no idea. All he knew is that something was wrong.
He'd stumbled down the road for what seemed like hours now. Images blasting into his head one after another. A series of horrid mental pictures that assaulted his mind: blood splattering all over the ceiling and walls, his girlfriend's face contorted in a scream, an gore covered axe slicing through the air. And he just

something was wrong...
