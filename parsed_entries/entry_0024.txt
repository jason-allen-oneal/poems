When a man is a shadow of what he once was, there is a prevailing blackness on his soul. A look in his eyes reveals a tortured existance. Within is turmoil and chaos. He feels as if there's nothing left for him to do with his life. His soul is so dark that not even the light of 10,000 suns can penetrate this devilish ebony power. This is darkness. An evil of mass destruction. A force so strong that even the world's biggest and strongest men cannot withstand it's constant, persuasive pull. The smallest can try and hide and hope it doesn't find them. The most holy are forced to turn away from their god or gods in its path. It leaves in its wake destruction, pain, and death. Such a darkness is slowly breaking in my heart. Evil is once again rearing its ugly head. There seems only one thing left to do...
