CONTENT:
I watch the moon trace it never-ending path across the sky as I stand here and smoke my second to last cigarette, thinking about how much my timeless celestial friend and I are alike.
We watch friends and enemies come and go. We've seen countless alliances grow, then dissolve like a grain of salt in the ocean.
Never seen.
Never appreciated.
Our presence seems meaningless at times. And yet, if one of us simply blinked out of existance one day, things would be... different. Changed somehow.
And this I take comfort in. Knowing that if I was not here tomorrow, that someone, somewhere would miss me. I did not believe that not so long ago. I didn't think anyone would care. Many times I've contemplated, and, a couple of times, actually tried, ending my life.
But now I know that people do care. Everyone we meet empowers us. Even the loneliest people have some sembalance (sp?) of friendship. And we should take heart in knowing that if we did vanish, at least one person in the long line of individuals we have interacted with over the years, would look back and say, "Hey... Remember that guy/gal? He/She was really cool. I miss him/her."
And so I say live each day to its fullest. Try and realize your dreams and goals instead of focusing on your failures. Be the best that you can be in whatever strikes your fancy. And remember that, somewhere in this cruel, pretentious world, someone loves you. Even if it is just a heavenly body.
And, to the moon, thanks for being there, and thanks for being and inspiration. I appreciate you even if no one else does.
