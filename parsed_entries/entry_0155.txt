The Dark Light

I have been here too long
for the memory of shadow to hold.
Even scent has learned to fade,
its edges scattered beyond reach.

The light is not bright—
only endless.
It moves without direction,
humming through the air
like dust that remembers flame.

I was afraid, once—
I braced for revelation.
But it arrived like snowfall,
and every thought
melted clean in its silence.

I do not shine.
I am carried in radiance.
The dark light feeds on clarity,
and I am what it has not yet finished understanding.

What drifts from me is lightened,
like language learning to forget itself.
When I speak, it rises slowly,
soft as breath surrendering to silence—
a sound with no mark,
only the hint of a breath
that may have belonged to someone else.

The light asks nothing.
That is its cruelty,
and its mercy.
It only reveals,
folding truth upon truth
until even meaning
becomes translucent.

Still, I stay.
Someone must remember
how it felt
to vanish honestly—
how beautiful the forgetting was.
