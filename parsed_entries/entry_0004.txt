When something is undiscovered, does that mean it doesn't exist? Or when it is discovered, does it magically come into being? Or vice versa so that it comes into being the second before it's discovered?
And if you don't believe in something, does that thing not exist for you? Say, for instance, you and a friend were in a haunted house. You don't believe in ghosts, but your friend does. So, this ghost appears, but only your friend can see it. Can you not see it because you do not believe in it, or can you friend see it because his mind is more open and the presence registers more in his mind.
Nex popint. Can you make something not exist simply by deciding not to believe in it? The answer is no. If there is concrete evidence that something is true, that it exists, you can't simply say "I don't believe in that," and it will go away. If I'm sitting in this chair, I have sufficient evidence that it does exist and that I can't wish it away or not believe in it to make it leave this reality. Besides, if one wished one's chair away, one's ass would be on the floor of one's bedroom.
But, the opposite of the last point is true. You can make something come into reality by believing it. Most people would call this either :excersizing one's will" or "having faith."
