CONTENT:
The Archivist

I was not taken with the rest.
The light passed through me
and kept going.

What remains here is quiet—
a horizon made of remnants,
each one whispering its last line.

I gather what survived illumination:
names half-dissolved,
sentences without subjects,
the warmth of forgotten gestures.

They settle in me like sediment,
glimmering when I move.
I tell myself this is purpose.
I tell myself the silence approves.

Sometimes the echoes call to each other
and the air folds,
re-creating the shape of their voices.
For a moment the world breathes again,
and I almost forgive myself
for wanting to hear it.

The dark light hums above—
patient, immaculate,
its gaze fixed on what I restore.
It knows these fragments will fade,
that I cannot preserve what has already agreed to vanish.

Still, I record.
Because loss deserves a witness.
Because even exile
can serve as memory’s last instrument.

And if the light returns,
let it find me here,
hands full of echoes,
ready to burn beautifully
one final time.
