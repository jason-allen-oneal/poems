CONTENT:
The Still Between Breaths

The sky leans closer at dusk,
listening for the names we never said aloud.
Light leaves its fingerprints on everything
and calls that proof of having lived.

The air tastes of rain and endings.
Even the crows sound like confession.

I walk where the river once was,
following the ghost of its current,
the memory of motion carved in permanence.

Something waits in the hush—
not cruel, just patient.
It has the weight of inevitability
and the gentleness of sleep.

When it comes,
I won’t mistake it for loss.
I’ll know it by the way
it remembers my name.
