The Fire
Smouldering passions are rekindled quickly
Flames of anxiety flicker into the night
Truths are laid bare in the warmth
And we stand naked here before the light
Ancient visions draw us from the present
Seeking to seduce our tribal senses with lust
A troubadour unto our aching souls
But will the song be one of trust?
"'Tis better to have loved and lost,
Than to have never loved at all"
Words used by those whom passion's longing embrace
Has never truly and fully enthralled
Exquisite torture of what was and what could be
Stoke the flames to lick the fractured sky
Dreaming of the past's transgressions
Chasing the nightmare from a lover's eye
