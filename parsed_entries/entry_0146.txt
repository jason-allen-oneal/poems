CONTENT:
unending, unknowing
a burning desire
doused as one would a flame.
smoke drifts through memory,
an echo where you used to be.
the death of a dream—
now knowing only agony.
fear moves like fog,
thick with the thought
that silence kept you.
i whisper your name
into the static between stars.
no word returns,
only the pulse of distance
and the regret of what i’ve lost.
i cry out again
but the echo comes back hollow—
a vessel cracked by longing.
the night whispers without mercy,
as the sky folds in upon itself
each thought of you
is another small extinction.
even memory forgets its shape.
what embers remain burn only
to remind me how cold the world has become.
and still i wait,
until even waiting dissolves—
until i, too,
am gathered
into the silence
that kept you.
