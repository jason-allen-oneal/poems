Resurrection
All the world has passed me by
With all its hollow hungers
Watched it all with dying eyes
As this life pulls me under
Breathe new life into the flesh
Staring forth from undead eyes
Wash away the bloody mess
And never stop to realize
Flowing now this putrid blood
Crawling out from the grave
Vengence seething in its brain
And nothing will be saved
Transformation of the soul
Brings about a stirring call
I seem to be the only who knows
The pride before the fall
Intoxicating proof of life
Blackened waters deep
Dream about a fruitless sky
And yet the innocence keep
Truth behind a thousand lies
Brings the hatred into view
Light the fires and let them blaze
And set the world askew
