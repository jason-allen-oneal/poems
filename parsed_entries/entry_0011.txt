This is a poem I wrote for my friend Trey that died in a car wreck the 24th of July.
Friend of all
Hated by none
Gone now
Are the days of fun
Time moves slow
And brings us pain
All seems lost
Here in the rain
But fear ye not
For it's not all over
Passed on to happiness
Among fields of clover
Hurt here we stand
In this sorrowful place
All of us thinking
"What's wrong with the human race?"
Life's taken us all
For a long, tumultuous ride
The path of which will be hard
Without you by our side
A lover, a friend
A person so dear
All theses words
We hope you can hear
Help us, dear friend
Be our guiding ray
To you we raise our glass
"This one's for Trey."
