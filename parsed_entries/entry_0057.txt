If you're reading this, then I'm already dead. I know there were a lot of things that I've left undone, and that's the only thing I really regret.
Which is a lie. I've never been able to put my past behind me. Things I should have said and done but never did. People I should have told more often that I loved them. But I didn't. And that, in my mind, makes me a bad person.
All my friends have abandoned me because, in some way or another, I mistreated them. The woman I love more than anything in the world despises my very existance.
Well, it's time to put everyone's worry to rest. This is goodbye. There won't be anymore getting high with friends. No more summer getaways. No more anything. It's over.
Ever shameful and remorseful,
Jason
