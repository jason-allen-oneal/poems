Darkness crept over the city. The boy was late... too late. He knew he should have been home by now. Now it was past dark. His eyes darted back and forth... seeing demons in every moving shadow.
There. What was that? Nothing. The boy sighed in relief. Still, his muscles were tensed and ready for action in the event of any sign of trouble.
But apparently not tensed and not ready enough...
He glanced toward another suspected lurker. Just in time to see the most horrid pair of eyes he had ever seen. Then there was darkness...
* * *
So, there I was, right? Hanging out in the alley as usual. Watching the people come and go, searching for that certain someone.
Couple of perks here and there... A VERY busty brunette, a fine, strong young man...
But finally, I saw him.
He couldn't have been older than 9. And he was afraid... very afraid. Perhaps that's what attracted my attention. I have heightened senses, you know?
Anyway, he was looking right and left, that petrified look upon his face. I started toward him... slowly. My lips curled into a smile. My hand clenched at the straight razor in my pocket. I pulled it out and opened the blade.
I think it was the glint of a streetlight reflecting off the blade as I held it against my leg that set the boy off. But it was already too late. His life blood flowed over my hand. My smile grew into a near-snarling grin.
I pulled his body back into the alleyway, the whole time swiping his sweet life across my face. I buried my face in the wound, sucking at and drinking the scalding blood that was spewing forth.
After I had glutted myself, I tossed the boy's body in a nearby dumpster.
It was time to move on to another city. THEY always catch on way too fast...
