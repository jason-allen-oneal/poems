Higher spirits
Of a thoughtless mind
Your greatest wish
You could never find
Put it away
And step up to bat
Push it down
Thoughts of this and that
Screaming in silence
A lonely, ragged cry
Joy is somewhat found
Within the midnight's sky
Breathe the scent of Jasmine
And taste her on my lips
With my own form of insanity
I have finally come to grips
Another useless poem
Written to a friend
A friend I know that
Could never be there in the end
