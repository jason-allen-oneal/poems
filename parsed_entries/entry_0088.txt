Electric blue
Fuschia and green
Lend their color
To a peaceful scene
The sun is right
And dipping low
Illuminating me
In its orange glow
The silver clouds watch
Standing side by side
The sun grows weary
And behind the clouds tries to hide
In a final show
The colors leap
To a dark hue
Changing deep
Finally color is gone
From the darkening sky
Why did it strike me so much?
Maybe because I'm high...
