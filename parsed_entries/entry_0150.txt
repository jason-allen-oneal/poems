Part 2: After the Ritual

The tower was gone.
The wind had merely performed
its ancient ritual.

The dust obeyed its cosmic whim,
as everything eventually did.

Guilt lingered like decay in the air—
not punishment, just residual.
It reminded me I was part of the ruined design.

Fate wasn’t cruel;
it was consistent.
It didn’t choose, it completed.

I no longer pleaded for reprieve.
The mirror and I had signed our truce:
it reflected, I endured.

If there was mercy,
it was in knowing the blade had been mine to lift,
and that I had finally stopped asking it to miss.
