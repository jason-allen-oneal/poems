TIMESTAMP:
5:08 PM - Apr 24, 2002

CONTENT:
I stare at the t.v. for another hour.
Mindless drivel, all of it.
Blood trickles down my face.
It covers my hands.
I look at the congealing, sticky liquid.
What happened?
I touch my face... my forehead and scalp.
No pain... except for the ringing in my head.
I look in the mirror.
My clothes are torn...
I'm soaked in blood.
Why?
I look back to the t.v.
Same shit.
Always the same shit.
I look around the room.
Nothing out of place.
What's wrong with this picture?
My cigarette burns in the ashtray.
The smoke curls upward until it hits the smog hanging above.
The cigarette is accompanied by several other butts.
How long have I been here?
I walk toward the kitchen...
Past the couch...
Past the cabinet cluttered with all her shit.
Why did she have to have only her stuff everywhere?
Into the kitchen doorway.
I almost slip and fall.
What the fuck?
I look down.
More blood... only it's all in one big pool.
A horrible image flashes in my mind.
A knife... blood splattering...
Things begin to come into focus.
I take another step and looked over.
She lay there on the floor.
Her eyes stared blankly at the ceiling as blood drained from her body.
I gagged.
What had I done?
The knife still protruded from the ripped flesh of her throat.
I fall to the floor.
GOD! Why?
There was a slight sucking/tearing sound as I pulled the knife free.
This can't be! Why? What happened?!
I clutch the knife to my chest as I rock back and forth.
No. Nonononononono...
I stare at the knife.
So much blood.
So much blood...
I bring the knife to my throat.
I pull.
I can feel the burn as the blade eats into my skin.
I feel the warmth as my blood spews forth over my hand.
I watch it as it mingles on the floor with her blood.
My eyelids sag.
My arms feel heavy.
I fall over... half on top of, half beside her.
I close my eyes.
Nononono..n...o...no............ ... ......  .....   ... .... ..   .   .        .
I die.
